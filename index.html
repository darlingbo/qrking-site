Questions people ask

Can I try a plan for free?

Yes. Your first 14 days on any plan are free, and you don’t need a card or MoMo to start. We remind you two days before the trial ends.

How do I pay?

With MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Visa or Mastercard. You approve every payment on your phone.

Can I pay every month instead of once a year?

Yes. Choose Monthly at the top of the page. Paying once a year costs less: up to 25% off.

What does “change a code after printing” mean?

Pro codes point to a QRKing link. You decide where that link sends people, and you can change it any time from your dashboard. The sticker on your counter never needs reprinting.

What happens to my codes if I stop paying?

Your codes keep working and keep sending people to the last place you set. You just can’t change them or see new scan numbers until you come back.

Can I move to another plan later?

Yes, any time. When you move up, you only pay the difference for the days left.

How do I cancel?

From your account, in two taps. Nothing renews after that.

Do you give discounts to churches, schools or charities?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us about your group.

Do free codes ever expire?

No. Free codes hold your information directly, so they work forever, even without QRKing.

Privacy and terms

Plain language, short, and honest about what we keep.

Draft for the prototype. Have it checked against Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) before launch.

What happens when you scan

Scanning happens on your own phone. The picture from your camera is never uploaded. Your scan history is saved only in your browser on that phone, and you can clear it from the History tab.

What we keep for free codes

Nothing. Free codes are made on your phone and hold your information directly, so we never see what you put in them.

What we keep for Pro

  • Your phone number or email, so you can sign in.
  • Your codes and where they point.
  • For each scan of a Pro code: the time, the town it came from (worked out from the network, not GPS) and the phone type. We don’t record who the person is.
  • Payment records from our payment provider. We never see your card or MoMo PIN.

Waitlist

If you join the Pro waitlist we keep your phone number or email only to tell you when Pro launches. Ask us and we’ll delete it.

We don’t sell your data

We don’t sell or rent personal information to anyone, and we don’t show ads.

Fair use

Don’t use QRKing codes to mislead people, collect passwords or payments under false pretences, or send people to harmful sites. We’ll switch off Pro codes that do.
